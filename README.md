# Easy Eats Planner

An ADHD-friendly meal planner that works in any browser, even offline. It decides meals for you, has a low energy mode, and builds your grocery list automatically.

**Open the app:** https://khalilsuccesslines-arch.github.io/easy-eats-planner/

## Features
- **Planning:** Decide For Me (plans the week in one tap), spin the wheel, theme nights, drag and drop, skip days
- **Today screen:** energy check-in, "Too tired to cook?" easy dinners, prep reminders
- **Food:** a Recipe Box with 60 starter meals, favorites, safe foods and your own photos
- **Shopping:** an automatic grocery list grouped by store section, plus pantry and restock tracking
- **Money:** a weekly grocery budget
- **Extras:** notes, 5 themes, backup and restore

## Your data
Everything is saved in your own browser on your device. Use **Settings → Your data** to back up.
